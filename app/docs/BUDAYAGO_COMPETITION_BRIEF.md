# Bhaswara — Competition Brief for BudayaGo International

## 1. Executive summary
Bhaswara is a culture-first language learning platform designed for Indonesia's regional languages, especially low-resource and endangered languages. The product combines offensive-value learning mechanics, AI pronunciation scoring, and local cultural immersion to create a practical and emotionally resonant learning experience.

The app does not only teach vocabulary. It helps users preserve language and culture by making daily practice fun, measurable, and community-driven. This is highly aligned with the values of cultural preservation, youth engagement, and digital education.

## 2. Problem statement
Many regional languages in Indonesia are under pressure due to modernization and the dominance of national languages. Young people are increasingly disconnected from their local culture, especially when learning tools feel generic, outdated, or not reflective of their identity.

At the same time, a practical digital education product must do more than provide static content. It must offer:

- tangible learning outcomes
- daily motivation through gamification
- culturally relevant content
- AI-powered pronunciation assessment
- measurable progress that schools and communities can trust

## 3. Solution
Bhaswara brings together three pillars:

1. Language learning through daily micro-lessons
2. AI-based pronunciation evaluation for local-language speech
3. Cultural immersion through local stories, expressions, attire, and heritage context

This makes the product both educational and identity-forming.

## 4. Differentiators
- Local language first, not English-first
- AI feedback grounded in pronunciation rather than generic translation
- culturally native design language instead of copy-paste gamification patterns
- school and community-friendly workflow for teachers, local institutions, and cultural communities
- strong retention design using streaks, XP, and reflection loops

## 5. MVP outcomes we are building
The implemented MVP already includes:

- mobile-first learning flow
- Sunda language starter content
- lesson and quiz interactions
- pronunciation scoring
- XP and streak system
- leaderboard and progress tracking
- culture content cards

This gives a credible prototype with clear room for expansion to additional regional languages and institutional usage.

## 6. Strategic positioning for international competition
The strongest story is not "we built an app". The strongest story is:

"We built a digital culture-preservation engine that makes endangered languages accessible, engaging, and useful for the younger generation through AI and gamification."

That is product, impact, and culture combined in a single narrative.

## 7. Roadmap to scale
Phase 1: Sunda starter + 30-50 words + teacher dashboard
Phase 2: 3-5 regional languages with voice scoring
Phase 3: school and community package for local government programs
Phase 4: regional-language corpus and cultural learning pathway
Phase 5: global showcase for cultural digital heritage and language revitalization

## 8. Impact metrics
We track meaningful indicators such as:

- D1 and D7 retention
- average practice sessions per week
- pronunciation score improvement over 4 weeks
- streak completion rate
- number of active schools and communities
- number of daily active learners

## 9. Why this matters
This project is more than a learning app. It supports identity, intergenerational continuity, local creativity, and digital inclusion. It makes technology work for cultural revitalization rather than viewing culture as content alone.

That is exactly the kind of initiative that stands out in a global cultural innovation competition.
