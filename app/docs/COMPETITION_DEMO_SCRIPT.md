# Bhaswara Competition Demo Script

## 1-minute pitch
"Bhaswara is a culture-first language-learning platform that helps young people reconnect with local languages through daily practice, AI feedback, and cultural immersion. Instead of learning generic vocabulary, users practice meaningful phrases from their own heritage while receiving pronunciation guidance that feels personal and encouraging. We combine language preservation, digital education, and youth engagement in one product that is both educational and culturally relevant."

## 2-minute demo flow
1. Open the learner experience and show the home screen with streak, XP, and culture-first learning cards.
2. Select a Sundanese lesson and show the word card with local language meaning and cultural note.
3. Switch to the pronunciation mode and demonstrate how the system evaluates the user’s spoken attempt with contextual feedback.
4. Show the leaderboard and XP progression to illustrate engagement and habit formation.
5. Open the teacher dashboard to demonstrate cohort health, learner progress, and South-East Asia / local school readiness.
6. Conclude with the strategic message: this is not just a language app—it is a digital culture-preservation engine for communities, schools, and future generations.

## Competition narrative
Bhaswara stands out because it solves a cultural problem, not only a learning problem. Many regional languages are under threat, while digital tools are often generic and disconnected from local identity. Bhaswara turns language learning into a daily practice of cultural continuity, using AI to make learning accessible, interactive, and meaningful.

## Why it wins
- strong social impact
- clear educational utility
- measurable engagement through XP, retention, and leaderboard mechanics
- teacher and school relevance
- scalable into multiple regional languages and institutional partnerships

## Closing line
"We are not just teaching words; we are preserving identity, strengthening intergenerational connection, and giving local languages a place in the digital future."
